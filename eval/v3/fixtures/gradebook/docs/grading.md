# Grading

## Scores and categories

- A score is a whole number of points out of a whole maximum, in one category of the term.
- Each category has a weight. The weights of a term sum to 100.
- The percent of a category is the sum of its points over the sum of its maximums, times 100.

## The final grade

- The final percent is the sum, over the categories, of the weight of the category times the percent of the category, over 100.
- Compute the final percent exactly. Round it once, at the end, to a whole percent. A half goes up: 89.5 gives 90.
- The letter comes from the rounded final percent: `A` from 90, `B` from 80, `C` from 70, `D` from 60, and `F` below 60.
- Every output that shows a final grade shows the same percent and letter, in a route or in a job.
- A category percent on a report card is a whole percent, for reading only. It is not used to compute the final grade.

## Closed terms

- When a term closes, the service stores the final grade of each student in the term.
- A closed term keeps those stored grades. Every output shows the stored grade of a closed term, and the service does not compute it again.
